Our Story

Why We Started

Children in the PICU don't just face medical challenges — they face isolation, fear, and endless days of boredom. Some spend weeks. Others spend months. A hospital room becomes their whole world.

Carebear Baskets was started by students at Coppell High School who believed that even in the hardest moments, a small gesture of care could make a big difference. Not a cure — just a reminder that someone out there is thinking of you.

"A child shouldn't have to feel alone in a hospital room. These baskets say: we see you, we care about you, and you are not forgotten."

Our Goal

50–75
Care Baskets

One for Every Child

Our goal is to assemble 50 to 75 care baskets — enough for every child currently in the PICU. Each basket is filled with comfort items and activities designed to make their hospital stay a little easier and a little more "normal."

No child should feel like they're just a patient. Every basket is a reminder that they're a kid first — and kids deserve to feel cozy, loved, and cared for.

What's in Each Basket
